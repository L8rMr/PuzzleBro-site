Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PuzzleBro doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no sign-ins, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Your photos stay on your device

When you make a puzzle from your own photo, you choose the photo with Apple's photo picker. PuzzleBro only receives the photo you pick, and saves a copy on your device so you can play it. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Your progress stays on your device

Puzzles in progress, best times, streaks and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Free photo library

To show real photos, PuzzleBro downloads pictures from Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org). Like any website, Wikimedia receives standard technical information needed to deliver them, such as your device's IP address. PuzzleBro doesn't send Wikimedia anything about you. See the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.

Children

PuzzleBro is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app.
